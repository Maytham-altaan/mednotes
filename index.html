Common questions

Where is my data stored?
Everything stays on your device. MedNotes has no account and uploads nothing.

How do backups work?
In Settings, create an encrypted backup secured by a password you choose, then save or share the file. Restoring adds its contents to your library. Keep the password safe — a backup can't be recovered without it.

I forgot my app password / can't unlock.
MedNotes unlocks with your device's Face ID, Touch ID, or passcode. If your device authentication works, the app will open.
